Your recipes

The best Kiwi recipes come from Kiwi kitchens.

Got a cheap, easy meal your family actually eats on a Tuesday? We would love to put it in the book, with your name on it, priced per serve at real supermarket prices. Fill this in and it opens an email to us with your recipe ready to send.

Attach a photo of the dish to the email if you have one. Your own photo, and your own recipe: nothing copied out of a book or off another website.

How you'll be credited in the app, e.g. "Recipe by Sam T, Hamilton".

Prefer to just write to us? Email info@churate.com with the recipe, your name, and a photo if you have one.

Partner recipes
