About prices

ChurAte collects prices from publicly available supermarket websites and shows them so you can compare. Those prices:

  • are collected periodically, so they can be hours or days old;
  • may differ from the price at the till, including because of in-store specials, loyalty pricing, regional differences and simple error;
  • may be matched to the wrong product, because matching thousands of supermarket listings to everyday ingredients is imperfect.

The app shows how old a store's prices are, and flags lines where the underlying data looks wrong. Please treat every total as an estimate.
