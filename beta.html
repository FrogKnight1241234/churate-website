What being a tester means

  • You get each new version as it ships, straight from us.
  • Everything works without an account, and the app never asks for your location or tracks you.
  • When a price looks off or something breaks, you tell us. There's a feedback button in the app that does the hard part.
  • It's free, and there's nothing to cancel. Stop any time by uninstalling.
