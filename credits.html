ChurAte · New Zealand

Most of the recipes in ChurAte are real home-cook recipes shared openly by their authors on Foodista under the Creative Commons Attribution licence. We adapt each one for New Zealand supermarket ingredients and metric quantities, and every recipe in the app links back to its original. Thank you to the cooks below.
