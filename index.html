A meal planner that knows New Zealand grocery prices — so you find out what dinner costs before the checkout, not after.

What it does

Turns a week of meals into one shopping list

Pick what you're cooking. ChurAte works out everything you need, subtracts what's already in your pantry, and groups the rest by aisle.

Prices it at the branches you actually shop at

Prices differ between stores of the same chain, so ChurAte compares your chosen branches and shows whether splitting the shop beats doing it all in one place.

Shows what a meal really costs per serve

A bag of oats isn't the cost of one bowl of porridge. Recipes show the cost per serving in ingredients, alongside what you'll actually pay at the till — the difference stays in your pantry for next time.

Imports recipes from a link or a photo

Paste a recipe URL, or photograph a page from a cookbook. The text is read on your phone and matched to real products. Nothing is uploaded.

Shares one list with your household

Create a household code, type it into another phone, and everyone sees the same plan and the same shopping list.

What it doesn't do

No advertising. No analytics. No tracking. It works perfectly well without an account, and it never asks for your location. Recipe photos are read on your device and never leave it. The privacy policy is short because there is genuinely very little to say.

About the prices

Prices are gathered from publicly available supermarket websites and are indicative only — they can be out of date, and matching thousands of supermarket listings to everyday ingredients is imperfect. The app shows how old each store's prices are and flags lines that look wrong, but always check in store before relying on a total.

ChurAte is an independent New Zealand app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any supermarket or grocery chain.
