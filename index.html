How it works

Three steps between "what's for dinner?" and the cheapest trolley.

1

Plan the week

Pick recipes — ours, one pasted from any website, or one photographed straight out of a cookbook. The photo is read on your phone and never uploaded.

2

Get one priced list

ChurAte merges every ingredient, subtracts what's already in your pantry, groups the rest by aisle, and prices it at your local branches.

3

Shop it the cheapest way

See whether splitting the shop across stores beats one big shop — or lock the list to a single supermarket and go.

Per serve, not per pack

A bag of oats isn't the price of one bowl of porridge.

Most apps show you the till total, which makes home cooking look expensive — a whole bag of flour against one cake. ChurAte shows what a meal actually uses, so you can compare recipes fairly. The rest isn't wasted money; it's next week's ingredients, sitting in your pantry.

Prices are gathered from publicly available supermarket websites and are indicative only — they can be out of date, and matching thousands of listings to everyday ingredients is imperfect. The app shows how old each store's prices are and flags lines that look wrong, but always check in store.
