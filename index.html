What it does

Three things, done properly.

1

Plan the week

Use our recipe book, paste a recipe from any website, or photograph one out of a cookbook. The photo is read on your phone and never uploaded.

2

Get one smart list

Every ingredient merged, what's in your pantry subtracted, the rest grouped by aisle and priced at the stores you actually shop at.

3

Shop it the cheapest way

See the cheapest way to buy the week, one big shop or a smart split, and tick things off as you go. Local stores you love can join the comparison too.
